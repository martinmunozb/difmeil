FAQ

Before you ask

Is it safe to give access to my Gmail?

Yes. You authorize through Google's official flow (OAuth) — Difmeil never sees or stores your password, and you can revoke access from your Google account whenever you want.

Do you store my emails on any server?

No. Difmeil has no servers of its own that store your mail: requests go straight from your browser to Google, and an email's body is only downloaded when you open it, never persisted anywhere.

How many accounts can I connect for free?

Up to 5. If you need more, or want advanced personalization and notification rules, that's where Pro comes in.

Does it work with Google Workspace accounts?

It works right now with personal Gmail accounts. Workspace support is finishing validation.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

You go back to the Free plan. The accounts you already connected keep working — access to your mail is never cut off for not paying.

Does it work in browsers other than Chrome?

Yes, in any Chromium-based browser — Edge, Brave, Opera — that supports Chrome Web Store extensions.
