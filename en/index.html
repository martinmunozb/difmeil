Questions before you install

Is it safe to give access to Gmail?

The connection uses Google’s official OAuth flow. Difmeil never sees your password, and you can revoke access from your Google account at any time.

Do you store my emails on a server?

No. Difmeil does not store your mail on its own servers. A message body downloads when you open it and is not persisted.

How many accounts can I connect for free?

Up to 3. You can use the unified inbox, read, search, reply and organize without paying.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

You return to the Free plan. Accounts that were already connected remain accessible, but you cannot add another while you have 3 or more. We never block your email because you stopped paying.

Does it work outside Chrome?

Difmeil is distributed through Chrome Web Store and can be installed in Chromium browsers that support the store, such as Edge, Brave or Opera.
