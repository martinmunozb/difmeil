Difmeil Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · Español · Français · Deutsch · Português

Difmeil is a browser extension designed to help the user manage and view their emails more comfortably from their own browser.

This privacy policy explains what data Difmeil may process, what it is used for, and what rights the user has.

Data Difmeil may process

Difmeil may access certain data from the user's Google/Gmail account, provided the user grants the necessary permissions through Google OAuth.

Depending on the features used, the extension may process:

  • The user's email address.
  • The list of emails.
  • Sender, recipient, subject, date and labels of the emails.
  • Read status, starred, archived or other organizational actions.
  • Email content only when necessary to display it to the user within the extension.
  • Basic technical data necessary for the extension to work.

Difmeil does not sell the user's personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

Purpose of processing

The data is used solely to:

  • Allow the user to view, organize and manage their emails.
  • Carry out actions requested by the user, such as marking emails as read, archiving, deleting or sending them.
  • Improve the experience of using the extension.
  • Maintain the security and correct operation of the service.

Use of Google data

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is carried out in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Difmeil only requests the permissions necessary to provide its features. Data obtained through Google APIs is not used for advertising, the sale of data, building commercial profiles, or transfer to third parties not necessary to provide the service.

Google permissions requested

For Difmeil to work correctly, the extension may request access permissions to the user's Google account through OAuth. These permissions are used only to carry out the functions the user requests within the extension.

Difmeil may request the following permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify: allows reading, organizing and modifying Gmail messages, for example marking emails as read, archiving them, applying labels or moving them when the user performs an action within the extension.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send: allows sending emails from the user's Gmail account only when the user expressly requests sending from Difmeil.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.other.readonly: allows read-only access to contacts and other Google contacts, to support features like recipient autocomplete or contact identification.

Difmeil does not use these permissions to sell data, show personalized advertising, build commercial profiles, or share information with third parties not necessary to provide the service.

Authentication tokens are managed securely and used only to maintain the session and carry out the functions authorized by the user. The extension's local configuration may be saved in chrome.storage.local to remember the user's preferences within the browser.

Data storage

Difmeil may store certain data locally in the user's browser to remember preferences, configuration or to improve the extension's operation.

Unless strictly necessary to provide the service, Difmeil does not store email content on its own servers.

When necessary for the extension's technical operation, Difmeil may use a secure backend function to complete the OAuth process and exchange authorization codes for access tokens. These tokens are used solely to authenticate the user and carry out the authorized actions.

Sharing data with third parties

Difmeil does not sell or rent personal data.

Data may only be shared when necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Provide the service requested by the user.
  • Maintain the technical security of the extension.
  • Use technical providers necessary for the service to operate, always under adequate protection measures.

Security

Difmeil applies reasonable measures to protect the user's data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or misuse.

Even so, no computer system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Data retention

Data will be kept only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or while the user keeps the extension installed and connected.

The user can revoke Difmeil's access to their Google account at any time from their Google account's security settings.

User rights

The user can exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability by writing to: support@difmeil.com

The user may also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos) if they believe the processing of their data does not comply with applicable regulations.

Minors

Difmeil is not specifically directed at minors. If a minor uses the extension, they must do so with the authorization of their parents, guardians or legal representatives where required.

Changes to this policy

Difmeil may update this privacy policy when necessary due to legal, technical or functional changes. The updated version will be available on the official privacy page.

Contact
